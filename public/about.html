Wellness just got sweeter.

Behind NutriBae

After being bedbound for a year from lupus and peripheral neuropathy, I learned something powerful: the right support can transform how we feel from the inside out.

Inspired by my journey of recovery, I created NutriBae — high-quality health supplements produced in FDA-registered and GMP-certified facilities in the USA, designed to help women feel healthier, empowered and strong.

What goes in every scoop

NutriBae protein is 100% plant-based, vegan, gluten-free, soy-free, lactose-free and non-GMO. It’s sweetened with stevia and low in sugar (just 1–4g of added sugar per serving), and made in an FDA-registered facility in the USA.

Every flavor is inspired by a dessert — Vanilla Birthday Cake, Chocolate Brownie Delight, Strawberry Shortcake — so staying on track feels like a treat, not a chore.

More than a shake

Every purchase can add a free digital copy of Wellness Made Sweet: 25 dessert-inspired smoothies, breakfasts and snacks made with NutriBae, all sugar-free, gluten-free and high-protein.

More than a brand

NutriBae isn’t just a brand — it’s a movement for women who want to take control of their wellness, glow from the inside out, and live life full of energy, no matter their journey.
