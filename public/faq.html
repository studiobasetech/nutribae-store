Quick answers about our products, shipping and orders.

Products

What is NutriBae protein made from?

A plant-based blend of fava bean protein isolate and YESTEIN™ fermented nutritional yeast protein, with MCT oil powder, apple pectin and stevia. It’s free from dairy, soy and gluten, gives you a complete amino acid profile, and is made in the USA.

How much protein is in a serving?

Vanilla Birthday Cake has 20g and Chocolate Brownie Delight has 21g of plant-based protein per serving, both with just 110 calories.

Is there sugar in it?

Very little. NutriBae protein is sweetened with stevia and has 4g (Vanilla Birthday Cake) or 1g (Chocolate Brownie Delight) of added sugar per serving. Our Energy and Hydration powders are sugar-free.

What’s in the Energy Powder?

Natural caffeine from green tea with L-theanine, taurine, B vitamins and electrolytes, in sugar-free Strawberry Shortcake and Cotton Candy flavors. It contains caffeine, so skip it if you’re sensitive to caffeine, and don’t have more than 5 scoops in 24 hours.

What’s in the Hydration Powder?

Electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium) plus B vitamins in a caffeine-free, sugar-free Peach Mango drink. Each jar holds 5.71 oz (162g).

What’s in the Probiotic 20 Billion?

A blend of 13 probiotic strains, including L. acidophilus, L. rhamnosus and B. lactis, in a vegetable capsule. Each bottle has 30 capsules, a one-month supply.

Do your products contain allergens?

Our protein powders are free from dairy, soy and gluten. The Energy and Hydration powders are made in a facility that also processes milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame. Check the full ingredient list on each product page.

Is NutriBae safe for everyone?

Our products are made for adults. If you’re pregnant or nursing, under 18, taking medication or have a medical condition, check with your doctor before using any supplement.

How to use it

How do I make a protein shake?

Mix two scoops with 6–8 oz of water, plant milk or your favorite drink. Shake or blend until smooth. For ideas beyond shakes, try the recipes in Wellness Made Sweet, our cookbook that’s free with any purchase.

How do I use the Energy and Hydration powders?

Energy: mix one scoop with 6–10 oz of water. Hydration: mix one scoop with 14–20 oz of water. Use the scoop inside the jar, adjust the water to taste, and finish an opened jar within 90 days.

How do I take the probiotic?

One capsule a day with a glass of water (6–8 oz).

How should I store my products?

Somewhere cool and dry, with the lid closed tightly. Powders are sold by weight, not volume, so some settling in the jar is normal. Don’t use a product if its safety seal is broken or missing.
